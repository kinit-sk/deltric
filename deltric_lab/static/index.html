A QUICK FIELD GUIDE

From a GIF to an explanation

  1. Choose a Gaia case. Open a recorded case or search all development data and record a fresh run.
  2. Find a change. Play the claims, jump to the first absorbed anomaly, or step through normal claims that match their destination's majority label. Split the canvas to compare ground truth.
  3. Follow the winning path. Click a point. See each edge’s original-space cost and the cumulative reach used.
  4. Check the competition. Queue attempts distinguish accepted ownership, skipped ownership, and over-budget hops. Future evidence is marked.
  5. Try an idea. Save a parameter experiment or reach sweep. Compare with the recorded default and keep observations in the notebook.
“Normal claimed” does not prove correct cluster assignment. Projected distances do not determine costs. Earlier pipeline frames show reconstructed memberships; the queue inspector concerns reassignment.
