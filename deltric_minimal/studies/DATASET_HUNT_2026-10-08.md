# DelTriC Benchmark Dataset Hunt — Verified Candidate List

**Date:** 2026-10-08 · **Method:** 12 parallel domain scouts searched the public web for real-world labeled datasets usable as DelTriC benchmarks (X = (n,d) float64, y = 0..k−1 clusters, −1 = anomaly). Every download URL was curl-checked (HTTP 200, sane size) or explicitly flagged; n/d/k and anomaly semantics come from each source's own docs/papers, cited per dataset. Spot-downloads went to /tmp and were deleted immediately (disk hygiene). Martin's constraints applied: **real labels only** (no synthetic/generated filler), natural anomalies preferred, origin tagged on every row, laptop/perun tier per dataset, fixed testing/holdout splits — 60 holdout / 29 training, frozen 2026-10-08 before any fitting. Cross-scout duplicates were merged; same-X/different-anomaly pairs are kept as separate rows and flagged. Rejected candidates are in the appendix.

**Independent spot-check (Muse, 2026-10-08):** 10 URLs re-checked live across domains (ADBench fault, UCI steel/gas, Zenodo IoT-23, COIL-100, HF 20newsgroups_embedded, Bioconductor golubEsets, GeoNames cities5000, OGLE OCVS, MNIST test mirror) — all HTTP 200. Verification claims hold.

**Legend:** origin = natural (real anomaly/fault labels) · natural_rare_class (genuinely rare class at natural rate) · constructed (class downsampled or designated by benchmark authors — tagged honestly). Tier: laptop = n≤20k AND d≤100; perun = bigger. Verification: ✅ spot-downloaded (bytes inspected) · ✅ live (curl HEAD 200) · 📄 docs (page/docs read, URL live-checked) · ⚠️ page-live (ODDS: Cloudflare blocked curl from this VM; pages read via text fetch).

**Headline counts:** 89 unique datasets — 38 laptop-tier, 51 perun-tier. **Split fixed 2026-10-08: 60 holdout / 29 training** (89 total). By origin: ~40 natural, ~30 natural_rare_class, ~19 constructed (a few "Martin's call" edge cases flagged in notes).

### Clustbench (beyond "other")
- **D1 Ecoli.** E. coli protein localization (Horton & Nakai; UCI 39; also clustbench-uci suite v1.1.0, spot-verified label distribution 143/77/52/35/20/5/2/2). X = 336×7; y: three rarest sites (omL/imL/imS, 9 pts, 2.7%) → −1, rest → 0..4. natural_rare_class. Tests whether Delaunay separates sparse genuine anomalies from dense clusters in low-d. `https://github.com/gagolews/clustering-data-v1/raw/v1.1.0/uci/ecoli.{data.gz,labels0.gz,txt}` (also `https://archive.ics.uci.edu/static/public/39/ecoli.zip`). License: research/cite; upstream CC BY 4.0 unconfirmed. Size: KBs. Laptop. Proposed: testing.
- **D2 MNIST digits (clustbench-mnist).** 70,000×784 flattened digits, human-labeled (LeCun/Cortes/Burges), label distribution spot-verified (balanced 1..10). No natural anomaly — construct by downsampling one digit to ~1–5%. Perun geometry/scale stress test (Delaunay in 784-d original space vs UMAP topology). `https://github.com/gagolews/clustering-data-v1/raw/v1.1.0/mnist/digits.{data.gz,labels0.gz,txt}` (14.7 MB). License: research-use per suite terms. Proposed: holdout.
